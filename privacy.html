Privacy Policy

Technology Organizer · Effective October 30, 2026

Technology Organizer ("the app") is made by Oliver Strubbe. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app on iPhone, iPad, Apple Vision Pro and Mac.

The short version

Technology Organizer does not collect, store or share any of your personal data. Everything you enter stays on your devices and in your own private iCloud account. The developer cannot see or access it.

Information you enter

The devices, serial numbers, notes, reminders and other details you add are saved on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, the app syncs this data between your devices using your private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit). That data is stored by Apple under your Apple Account and is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy. The developer has no access to your iCloud data.

You can delete your data at any time by deleting items in the app or deleting the app. To remove synced data from iCloud, go to Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Account Storage on your iPhone or iPad.

Camera

The app can use your camera to scan serial numbers. The image is processed entirely on your device to read the text. Camera images are not saved, uploaded or shared. You can turn camera access off at any time in Settings.

Notifications

Reminders you set are scheduled as local notifications on your device. They are not sent through any server, and no notification data leaves your device.

Import and export

You can import and export your data as CSV files. Files are only created or read when you choose to, and they go only where you send them.

No tracking, analytics or advertising

The app has no accounts or sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. It does not use your data to track you across apps or websites.

Children's privacy

Because the app does not collect personal data, it does not knowingly collect information from children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country).

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
